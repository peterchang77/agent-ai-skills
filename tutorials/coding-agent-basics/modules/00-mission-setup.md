# Act 0 — Accept the Reporting Mission

## Situation

A team needs a draft report from monthly CSV exports. The raw files are evidence: they must remain unchanged. Duplicates, missing values, and unclear records must be visible to a person rather than silently “fixed.”

You will use the fictional sample project unless you explicitly choose an authorized copy of your own work.

## Your move

Tell the agent which mission boundary to use. Include all three parts:

1. the outcome you want;
2. what must not happen; and
3. whether to use the sample project or an authorized copy.

A concise command starter, if useful:

> Help me create a reviewable draft report from the sample project. Do not change raw data or publish anything.

## Agent mode

The agent records the learner profile, creates `.tutorial/mission-log.md` and `.tutorial/progress.md`, and names the first obstacle. It should not inspect project files until you direct a read-only inspection in Act 1.

## Inspect

Open the mission log. Confirm it names the outcome, safe boundary, route, and next learner move. Correct any condition that would be unsafe or incomplete in your real work.

## Unlock

A coding-agent tutorial works best when it has a practical mission, a protected boundary, and visible artifacts. The agent adapts its level of detail from how you direct the mission—not from a scored vocabulary quiz.

## Checkpoint

Act 0 is unlocked when the mission log states a safe outcome and boundary.
